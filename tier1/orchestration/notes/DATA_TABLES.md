### B1 — Map leak: process-state discrimination (tc=64)
| run | n | fit | R² | first 200 | last 200 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| single (6000) | 6000 | 1879 + 3.269·n | 0.984 | 2748 | 21031 |
| splitA (3000) | 3000 | 1941 + 3.185·n | 0.978 | 2793 | 11159 |
| splitB (3000, fresh process after splitA) | 3000 | 1830 + 3.326·n | 0.976 | 2667 | 11675 |
| rebuild every 220 | 3000 | 2541 + -0.076·n | 0.008 | 2536 | 1972 |

**Discriminator:** splitB starts at 2,668 µs (cold). If drift persisted across
processes it would start at 11,899 µs — where `single` sits at dispatch 3000.
**Rebuild every 220 flattens the slope to −0.076 (R²=0.008): the leak is in the Map.**
